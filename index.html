5-minute presentation flow

1
Frame the operating problem

Show the dashboard as one source of truth across product, stock, sales and finance.

2
Demonstrate control

Open Inventory, initiate a transfer, then approve it to show segregation of duties.

3
Simulate a transaction

In Sales / POS, add a product and complete payment. KPIs and stock recalculate instantly.

4
Close the loop

Enter a declared payment total in Reconciliation and review the automatic variance.

5
Prove accountability

Use the Audit trail to show who did what and when, then switch roles to show access control.

Proof-of-concept boundary: this prototype demonstrates the workflow and intended controls. A production implementation would require authenticated identity, a secured database, maker-checker enforcement, integrations, backups and formal security testing.
